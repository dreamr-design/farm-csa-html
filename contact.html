Thursday is pickup day

Crates are packed by noon and waiting in the barn cooler from 3 to 7 pm. Town members pick up at the same hours in all six towns.

  • Bring last week's crate back, rinsed if you can.
  • Running late? Call the shed and we will leave yours in the cooler overnight.
  • Away for a week? Tell us by Monday and we donate your crate to the Viroqua pantry.
