Asked at the pickup table

Questions, answered

Something missing? Write to Juniper at hello@bramblecut.farm. She answers after chores.

What if I am away one week?

Tell us by Monday. We skip that week and add its value to a later crate, or you can send a friend to pick it up in your name. Swaps per share are listed in the table above.

Can I choose what goes in my crate?

Not the crate itself; that is the deal with a CSA. Every pickup has a swap bin, though: leave the beets, take the extra kale somebody else left.

What happens if the weather ruins a crop?

You share that risk with us, and the good years too. A wet June might mean fewer peas and more lettuce. In fourteen seasons we have never sent out an empty week.

Where are the pickups?

Six spots every Thursday: the farm itself from 3 to 7 pm, the Viroqua food co-op, Westby, Coon Valley, and two porches in La Crosse. Exact addresses come with your welcome letter.

Is everything certified organic?

Every acre, since 2014. The eggs come from the Hendersons up the road, who are certified too. The orchard fruit comes from a neighbor in transition, and the weekly note says so.

Can two households split one share?

Plenty do. A full share splits well between two small households. Put one name on the account, then trade weeks or halve the crate at pickup.
